2025 UCSD Postdoc PITCH Competition

This was the inaugural “Three Minute Thesis”-style competition for postdocs at UC San Diego. I talked about my research on endogenous viruses in cephalopods. I placed first in this competition.

2025 Torrey Pines Training Consortium (TPTC) Postdoc Pitch Competition

This was the mesa-wide follow up to the UC San Diego competition. The top two finalists from UC San Diego, Scripps Research, Salk Institute, and Sanford Prebys were selected as contestants. I came in second place at this competition, and also won the People’s Choice Award.
