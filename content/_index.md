### Why a personal website?
I personally wanted to have more of an online presence that doesn't depend on social media. Also, traditional metrics for evaluating scientists - number of publications, journal impact factor, number of citations, ... - perpetuate many systemic biases. So, I wanted to maintain an academic website as a way to build a holistic, up-to-date portfolio of my scientific interests and contributions that go beyond just my list of publications, to include teaching, mentorship, outreach, and more.

### How did I make this website?
In the spirit of using free and open source resources, I used [Hugo](https://github.com/gohugoio/hugo) to generate the website, using the theme "Anatole", customizing it to suit my aesthetic. The website is hosted at GitHub, and can be forked to use as a starting point for your own website, if you'd like.
