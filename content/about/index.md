## Who I Am -- The Bureaucratic Version
I am a postdoctoral fellow in Loren Looger's lab at the University of California, San Diego (UCSD). I received my PhD from the National Centre for Biological Sciences (NCBS-TIFR), Bangalore, India. Under the guidance of Dr. Vatsala Thirumalai, I studied the neural circuits controlling motor function in larval zebrafish (_Danio rerio_). More specifically, I investigated the implications of bistability in cerebellar Purkinje neurons for circuit function.
